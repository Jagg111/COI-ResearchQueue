How to install Research Queue
=============================

1. Locate your COI data folder. By default it is at:
   %APPDATA%\Captain of Industry

2. Open or create the "Mods" directory inside it.

3. Extract the contents of this zip into the "Mods" directory.
   You should end up with a folder structure like:
   Mods\ResearchQueue\manifest.json
   Mods\ResearchQueue\ResearchQueue.dll

   The folder name must match the mod ID (ResearchQueue).

4. Launch the game, but do NOT click Continue -- click Load instead.

5. Click the "Mods & DLCs" button, enable Research Queue, then load your save.

Once in-game, open the research tree. The queue panel will appear when no
research nodes are selected.

In case of any errors, examine logs in:
   %APPDATA%\Captain of Industry\Logs
